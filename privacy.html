Privacy policy

rclone-openclaw is a personal backup tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive solely to store and restore the owner's backups.

No data is collected, sold, shared with or transferred to any third party. No analytics or tracking is used. Data accessed through Google APIs stays in the owner's Google account.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
